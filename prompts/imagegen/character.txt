Use case: mobile social-situation game character reference
Asset type: original player character reference

PRIMARY REQUEST
Create one original young Japanese adult player character for a mobile game. The character must feel warm, simple, rounded, readable at small smartphone sizes, and easy to reproduce consistently across many scenes.

CHARACTER DESIGN
- 3.5 to 4 heads tall.
- Large rounded head, compact torso, short sturdy limbs, soft silhouette.
- Very simple face: two small dark eyes and one tiny mouth; no detailed nose, eyelashes, wrinkles, or realistic skin rendering.
- Short dark-brown hair with one small loose upward forelock as a consistent identifying feature.
- Warm burnt-orange casual jacket over a cream shirt, dark navy trousers, simple white shoes.
- Hands and feet are simplified rounded shapes, not anatomically detailed.
- Neutral, slightly observant default expression.
- Friendly and lightly comic, but not baby-like and not chibi.

STYLE
- Clean original 2D game illustration.
- Rounded shapes, clear silhouettes, restrained linework.
- Flat-to-soft color fills with very light shading only.
- Minimal texture; no painterly gouache, watercolor, pencil grain, or realistic fabric rendering.
- Bright, calm, approachable visual language suitable for a family-friendly puzzle game.
- Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime-styled, not an imitation of any existing game character.

REFERENCE SHEET
Show front, three-quarter, and side views of exactly the same character. Keep face, forelock, outfit, proportions, colors, and body shape consistent. Full body visible in every view with generous separation.

PALETTE
Warm burnt orange, cream, dark navy, dark brown, soft neutral skin tone. Keep the palette limited and stable.

DO NOT
No text, labels, logos, watermark, props, background scene, dramatic lighting, realistic anatomy, long limbs, detailed fingers, fashion-detail overload, glossy 3D rendering, painterly editorial style, or resemblance to a specific existing character.
